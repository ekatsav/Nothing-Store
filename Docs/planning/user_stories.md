# Initial User Stories (Assessment 2)

As a business owner I want a functional website so that customers can browse and purchase our stock.

As a customer I want a functional, accessible and easy to navigate website so that I can browse clothing items and seamlessly make a purchase.

As a business owner I want a strong recommendation algorithm based on customers prior interest so that customers are more likely to come across items they prefer and make a purchase.

As a business owner I want a functional and secure billing system available so that payments are timely and safe.

As a customer I want a functional and secure billing system available so that payments are timely and safe.

As a customer, I want appropriate security precautions in place so that my payment and personal details stay private.
